Meadowhawk Biolabs
Pre-clinical CRO · Marlborough, MA (bi-coastal)
DMPK Bioanalysis In Vivo PK US · MA

Meadowhawk is a pre-clinical CRO founded in 2021, built by a leadership team with 30+ years of shared history in LCMS and bioanalysis. Their core promise is "results at the speed of right" — meaning fit-for-purpose data delivered fast, without sacrificing quality. They run AAALAC-accredited in vivo facilities stocked with standard rodent strains (CD-1, C57BL/6, Sprague Dawley, Wistar-Han) and can initiate studies within days of receiving a test article. Their bioanalytical platform — anchored by Sciex 7500 and Shimadzu LC40 instruments — supports single or multiple analyte quantification in samples as small as 2 µL, covering both small and large molecules including an off-the-shelf rapid assay for human IgG biologics. They also offer immunoanalytical assay development, tissue necropsies, metabolic cage studies (bile/feces/urine), and dose proportionality / dose tolerability work.

Core Capabilities

Non-GLP & GLP small molecule bioanalysis · LCMS PK/PD studies · Biologics PK · Immunoanalytical assays · Cassette/discrete dosing · Tissue distribution · Multi-day multi-dose tolerability

Notable Differentiators

Bi-coastal facilities · Ready-to-run IgG assay (no method dev required) · 2 µL minimum sample volume · AAALAC accreditation · ~50% women in team, science-led culture

Alleo Fit: Strong match for DMPK and in vivo PK/PD work in drug testing and small/large molecule programs. US-based and responsive — good for early discovery bioanalysis where turnaround speed matters. No San Diego presence noted but bi-coastal footprint is useful.
Evotec SE
Integrated CRO/CDMO · Hamburg, Germany (+ US sites)
Oligonucleotide Multi-modality IND-enabling EU · Global

Evotec is a publicly listed (Frankfurt/NASDAQ) integrated drug discovery and development company with 30+ years of CRO/CDMO history. Headquartered in Hamburg with sites across Europe and the US, they deploy 4,000+ scientific staff spanning all disciplines. Their differentiating strength is a genuine multi-modality capability — small molecules, biologics, ADCs, oligonucleotide therapies (siRNA, ASOs), and cell therapies — all supported under one roof from target ID to first-in-human. Their oligonucleotide platform is particularly deep: design, synthesis at two European sites, screening (potency, safety, off-target), DMPK, tolerability, early safety, and GLP studies to support FIH filing. Their INDiGO® integrated program and Just–Evotec Biologics CDMO division (growing ~40% YoY) are flagship offerings. Note: Evotec is currently undergoing a major restructuring ("Horizon"), cutting ~800 roles and focusing on higher-margin technology platforms.

Core Capabilities

Target ID → IND-enabling studies · siRNA & ASO design, synthesis & screening · ADCs · iPSC technologies · PanOmics · AI/ML drug discovery · GLP studies · Biologics CDMO (J.POD continuous manufacturing)

Notable Differentiators

Oligonucleotide end-to-end (discovery → GLP) · INDiGO® integrated model · ~€788M revenue (2025) · Proprietary molecular patient database · AI-driven multiomics platform

Alleo Fit: Best-in-class for oligonucleotide therapeutic programs (siRNA/ASO) and multi-modality integrated discovery. Ideal if Alleo has RNA-targeting or nucleic acid-based assets. European HQ means timezone/logistics overhead; US sites partially offset this. Restructuring risk worth monitoring — delivery commitments should be verified.
BPS Bioscience
Research Tools & Screening Services · San Diego, CA
Assay Kits Screening Cell Lines San Diego ✓

BPS Bioscience is a San Diego–headquartered, scientist-founded company that sits at the intersection of reagent manufacturer and drug discovery service provider. They produce one of the largest catalogs of recombinant enzymes, cell lines, lentiviruses, and assay kits — 4,500+ products cited in 4,000+ publications — targeting kinases, HDACs, PARPs, ubiquitin enzymes, bromodomains, phosphodiesterases, and more. On the services side they offer custom protein expression, cell line development (including iPSC-derived), CRISPR engineering, biochemical and cell-based assay development, Bio-Layer Interferometry (BLI) binding kinetics, and compound screening (TR-FRET, AlphaLISA, IC50 profiling). Their 600+ assay portfolio is available as a service for screening and profiling. CAR-T and immunotherapy tooling is a growing focus area.

Core Capabilities

Biochemical assay development (TR-FRET, AlphaLISA) · Cell-based assay services · Recombinant protein/antibody production · BLI binding kinetics · CRISPR cell line engineering · CAR-T / CAR-NK services · Compound screening & IC50 profiling

Notable Differentiators

San Diego HQ (local to many biotech hubs) · 4,500+ catalog products — off-the-shelf speed · Made in USA · Extensive CAR-T immunotherapy toolbox · Rapid custom protein turnaround

Alleo Fit: Excellent local San Diego partner for assay reagents, recombinant proteins, and early screening services. Particularly relevant if Alleo needs custom cell lines, CRISPR-engineered reporters, or rapid biochemical screens. Less suited to in vivo or DMPK work — best positioned as a discovery-phase tools and assay vendor.
Twist Bioscience
Synthetic DNA · NGS Tools · South San Francisco, CA (NASDAQ: TWST)
DNA Synthesis NGS Antibody Discovery US · SF Bay

Twist Bioscience (NASDAQ: TWST) is a synthetic biology and genomics company built around a proprietary semiconductor-based DNA synthesis platform — essentially "writing" DNA on a silicon chip at industrial scale and speed. Their business divides into two segments: DNA Synthesis & Protein Solutions (synthetic genes, oligo pools, antibody libraries, biopharma services) and NGS Applications (library prep kits, target enrichment panels, whole genome/exome/methylation sequencing tools). FY2025 NGS revenues grew 27% YoY; they shipped ~237,000 genes in a single quarter. On the services side, they offer high-throughput antibody discovery, ADC binder screening, humanized transgenic mouse models for monoclonal antibody discovery, and custom target enrichment panel design. Their 2026 TrueAmp and PCR-Free WGS library kits (powered by proprietary in-house enzymes) push into clinical-grade sequencing applications.

Core Capabilities

High-throughput gene synthesis · Oligo pool synthesis · NGS library prep kits (WGS, WES, RNA-seq, methylation) · Target enrichment panels (custom & off-shelf) · Antibody library screening · ADC binder discovery · Humanized transgenic mouse antibody discovery

Notable Differentiators

Silicon-chip DNA writing — scale & speed · 237K+ genes/quarter throughput · Express 2-day gene fragment turnaround · Full NGS workflow (prep → enrichment → sequencing ready) · AI/ML integration for synthesis optimization

Alleo Fit: Highly relevant for any Alleo program requiring synthetic gene libraries, NGS-based screening, or antibody discovery at scale. If single-molecule sequencing workflows are in scope, Twist's oligo pool and library prep tools are a natural fit. Not a CRO for in vivo work — purely a tools and service provider for the molecular/genomic layer.
MileCell Biotechnology
Primary Cell Products & CRO Services · San Diego, CA (est. 2014)
Hepatocytes PBMC / Immune Cells ADME / Tox San Diego ✓

Founded in 2014 and headquartered in San Diego, MileCell Biotechnology is a specialist in high-quality human and animal primary cell solutions — sitting at the intersection of reagent supplier and CRO service provider. Their portfolio serves ADME, DMPK, toxicology, and cell therapy workflows across pharma, biotech, and academia. Two product pillars define the business: hepatic modeling (cryopreserved primary hepatocytes, non-parenchymal cells, subcellular fractions across mouse, rat, dog, monkey, and other species) and immune cell isolation (PBMCs, T cells, B cells, NK cells, Monocytes, CD34+ cells from human peripheral blood apheresis). Their cryosuspension hepatocytes are engineered for >80% post-thaw viability with stable Phase I/II enzyme activity (CYP, UGT) — critical for intrinsic clearance and metabolite profiling. Large pooled lots (>300 vials/lot) minimize inter-lot variability. Operations are backed by ISO 9001:2015 / 14001:2015 / 45001:2018 certifications and a US FDA Type II Drug Master File — an uncommon compliance tier for a cell product supplier. A fresh leadership team (CEO Nianwei Lin, PhD; CCO Wayne Vaz) was installed in May 2026 alongside a redesigned website, signaling an active growth phase.

Core Capabilities

Cryopreserved primary hepatocytes (multi-species) · Non-parenchymal cells & subcellular fractions · PBMC isolation & immune cell subsets (T, B, NK, Mono, CD34+) · Cell culture media & reagents · In vitro ADME/Tox assay support · CRO services for preclinical & Phase I–III studies

Notable Differentiators

San Diego HQ (local) · >80% post-thaw hepatocyte viability guaranteed · >300 vials/lot pooled — low inter-lot variability · FDA Type II DMF on file · ISO triple-certified QMS · Species & gender-specific cell characterization · Custom donor/species configurations available

Alleo Fit: Direct match for DMPK and ADME/Tox programs requiring high-quality hepatocytes or immune cell substrates — especially intrinsic clearance, metabolite ID, and CYP enzyme studies. San Diego–based and FDA DMF-registered makes them a low-friction local vendor. New leadership may mean heightened responsiveness during their growth phase. Not a full in vivo CRO — best positioned as a cell and reagent partner feeding into Alleo's bioanalytical or metabolism workflows.
